## 1. Purpose

This template provides the structured agenda and recording format for ISMS management reviews as required by ISO 27001 Clause 9.3. Management reviews ensure that the ISMS remains suitable, adequate, and effective, and that information security is aligned with the organization's strategic direction.

For a startup of CyberForge's size, management reviews are conducted semi-annually. This cadence balances ISO 27001 compliance (which requires reviews "at planned intervals") with the operational pace of a growing organization.
